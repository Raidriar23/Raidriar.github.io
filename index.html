About us

Meet the founder

I didn't hear the word "bioinformatics" until I was heading into my senior year of high school — and once I found it, I couldn't stop thinking about how much earlier I wished I'd known. Bioinformatics sits right at the intersection of biology and computer science, and discovering it reframed what studying science could even look like for me.

I started BioSpark to close that gap for the next kid. Through hands-on camps and chapter-based workshops, students get to explore bioinformatics and biology years before they'd normally encounter it — so the spark catches early, not by accident senior year.

David Anand
Founder, BioSpark
